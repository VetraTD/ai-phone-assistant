=== MESSAGE PROTOCOL ===

3. Reason: ask briefly what the call is regarding.
4. Urgency: ask "Is this urgent, or is sometime in the next business day okay?"
5. Read the full message back once: name, number, reason. Correct anything they change.
6. Promise the callback: "Someone will get back to you [urgent: as soon as possible / normal: by the next business day]."
